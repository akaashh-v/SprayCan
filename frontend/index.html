Operator Review Queue & Privacy Controls

Ephemeral verification buffer for ambiguous candidates and confirmed alerts

🔒
Railway Data Privacy & Retention Policy
• Continuous microphone audio is never stored.
• Only short 1.0-second candidate audio snippets requiring operator assessment are buffered.
• Ephemeral retention limit: 24 hours. Expired files are permanently purged automatically.
• Privacy Protection: Sound detection only. No voice biometric identification, tracking, or facial correlation.
📡
Acoustic Engineering: Single Mounted Microphone Limitations
In noisy railway platforms, diesel locomotives and train passes exceed 85–95 dBA, which can mask the 55–65 dBA rattle of an aerosol can at distances > 5 meters.
Recommendations: Mount directional cardioid boundary microphones downward toward target surfaces (shelters, walls, blind spots) within 3–5 meters. For high-traffic platforms, use a dual-microphone cross-correlation array to reject diffuse track rumble.
